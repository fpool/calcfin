About CalcFin

CalcFin is a collection of fast, free, no-nonsense financial calculators. Every calculator runs entirely in your browser — your numbers never touch a server — and every page explains the formula behind the result.

Why CalcFin exists

Most finance calculator sites bury the tool under ads, require signups, or hide the math. We do the opposite: clean tools, transparent formulas, honest estimates.

Principles

  • Local by default. No inputs ever leave your device.
  • Transparent math. Every page shows the exact formula used.
  • Free means free. No signup, no paywall, no limits.
